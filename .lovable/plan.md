# Drumil Publications Storefront

Build a polished, mobile-friendly storefront prototype for Drumil Publications based on the uploaded brief. The user chose the full storefront scope.

## Experience
- Replace the starter page with a branded editorial-style home screen featuring the supplied positioning, featured books and EyeCue games, and direct paths into shopping and the interactive lab.
- Create dedicated Shop, product detail, Interactive Lab, Mind Journal, Our Story, and Contact pages. Include useful filters/search, product previews, a short matching quiz, a playable focus mini-game, a daily insight with share action, and the supplied contact and bulk-order information.
- Add a cart drawer with quantity controls, shipping threshold, and coupon interaction, followed by a guest checkout demonstration and order confirmation. Clearly treat checkout/payment as a prototype: do not collect or transmit real payment details or claim an order has been placed with a real fulfillment service.
- Reuse catalog names, descriptions, and prices from the brief. Do not invent real customer reviews, purchase activity, author/founder claims, or fulfillment guarantees.

## Design
- Use a distinctive contemporary Indian publishing identity: crisp editorial typography, vivid saffron and green accents balanced with ink-dark text and clear light surfaces, thoughtful book/game imagery, compact controls, and responsive layouts.
- Keep components and colors aligned with the existing semantic token system; use the project's TanStack Start routing rather than the React Router architecture named in the uploaded prompt.

## Technical approach
- Implement file-based routes with page-specific SEO metadata. Use shared storefront navigation and a reusable, typed product catalog.
- Keep cart and lightweight visitor preferences in browser storage; no backend, account system, real payment provider, or live order submission is included in this prototype.
- Validate the main shopping-to-demo-checkout path, responsive presentation, route metadata, and project build/test signals.
